The Strategy Playbook

Systematic rules executed automatically every session.
1
09:25 AM CPR Barrier Gate
Evaluates 15m structural trend & Central Pivot Range (CPR). If Spot > Top Central (TC), initiate Bull Put Spread. If Spot < Bottom Central (BC), initiate Bear Call Spread. If inside CPR, stand aside (Capital Preserved).
Rule: Invalidation outside CPR
2
09:30 AM Dual Leg Execution
Sell OTM strike directly behind the CPR barrier (~₹20–25 premium). Simultaneously buy 50-pt further OTM wing (~₹8–10 premium) as a hedge. Margin drops from ₹1.35L to ₹32k/lot with zero unlimited tail risk.
Rule: Always Hedged
3
Strict Strike Breach Stop Loss
If NIFTY Spot touches the sold strike at any moment intraday, the position is immediately closed. Loss is strictly capped at ~22 pts (~₹2,860 on 2 lots). No hoping or averaging down.
Rule: Zero Overnight Risk
4
15:10 PM Target Square-Off
Both spread legs are closed at 15:10 PM to realize accumulated theta decay (+12 to +18 pts). We never carry positions overnight, eliminating gap-up / gap-down vulnerability.
Rule: Intraday Only
5
Multi-Channel Instant Alerts
Real-time entry, stop loss, and exit push notifications are dispatched instantly to your macOS Desktop Banner, Mobile Telegram Bot, and Email Inbox.
Rule: Desktop + Mobile
6
Scale Gracefully
Start with 2 lots (130 Qty) to build consistency. Once confident, effortlessly toggle SPREAD_DEFAULT_LOTS=5 in .env for 5-lot scaling without changing any other parameter.
