Market Map Levels

Shows your generated key levels inside Dhan DEXT, from the same snapshot the live-market website reads.

Must match the instrument on your Dext chart. NIFTY for the NIFTY 50 spot chart; the dated contract (e.g. NIFTY25AUG26FUT) for a futures chart. They sit ~40–70 pts apart, so the wrong one is wrong on every entry and stop.

These levels are areas, not entry lines. The card mirrors what your pipeline published for the session shown at its foot — it does not recompute anything, so if the map is stale the card says so rather than hiding it. Dhan charts cannot render custom Pine Scripts, which is why this is a side panel and not lines on your candles.
